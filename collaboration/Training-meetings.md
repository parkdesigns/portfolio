# Training Meetings

Occassionaly I would schedule `Training` meetings.

* Run tool exercises
* Teach new tool

## Occurrences

* `30+` Training meetings
  * `10+` attendees (typically)
  * `60+` attendees (rarely)

## Attendees

* Team Leads
* Developers
* DevOps

## Apps

* Outlook M365 Group Calendar
  * Meeting
  * Date/time
* Slack
  * Reminder with Webex link
* Webex
  * Screen sharing

## Example Topics

(list is NOT exhaustive)

1. Journey Availability data, calculation, & formula (1x 90 min session)
    * Excel spreadsheet of data sources, math calculations, and formula
    * Screenshots of Splunk Availability dashboard
    * Demo of Splunk Availability dashboard
2. Journey Availability dashboard, scorecard, (3 - 4x 60 min sessions)
    * Extensive screenshots
    * Example incidents and the availability impacts seen on dashboard
    * Senior Leadership scorecard derived from dashboard
    * Explanation of all metrics and sources involved
    * Spreadsheet of metrics and sources
3. Calling Exercise
    * Cisco Jabber
    * WhatsApp (fallback)
    * Ran exercises in calling international numbers
    * From India to US
    * From US to India
    * Beneficial for an incident or important matter requiring immediate contact
4. Paging Exercise
5. Monitoring Dashboard Exercise
    * Splunk
    * OpenSearch
    * Dynatrace
    * Grafana
    * Prometheus
    * Jaeger
6. DevOps RCA & Resolution Exercise
7. Demonstration of how-to use new Contact Center thick client
    * Demo (live) of using app in test environment
8. Email UX/UI Design (2x 60 min sessions)
    * Email Envelope
      * Headers
      * Subject
      * etc
    * Email Body
      * HTML
        * CSS styling
        * Preheader text
        * Base64 inline images versus hosted images
      * Attachments
    * UX/UI Design
      * Graph Design Principle of visual hierarchy
      * Readability
      * Templating
9. `curl`, `pbrun`, `openssl`, etc Command line tools
