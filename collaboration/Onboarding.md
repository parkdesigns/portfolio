# Onboarding

>_I have never had the pleasure of being onboarded to a company or a team. But I have always tried to build onboarding materials for those who would follow. -Steven Park_

## Scenarios

### No Onboarding

The scenario of no onboarding is common.

Aspects

* Team
  * no team defined other than team directory
* Role & Responsibilities
  * no explanation of role & responsibilities
* Application
  * no documentation
  * no test scripts
  * no mockups
  * no diagrams
  * no app development setup
  * no access to source code
  * no details in config mgmt
  * no details in change requests
* Business
  * inaccessible documentation
  * divide existing between Technology and Business people
