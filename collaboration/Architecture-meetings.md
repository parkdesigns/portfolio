# Architecture Meetings

In mid-2020 I started hosting and presenting:

* Architecture Review meetings

## Timings

|  |  |
| :-------- | :-------- |
| Occurence | Every 2 weeks |
| Duration | 30 min - 60 min |
| Timezone | late IST, early MST |

## Attendees

* Team Lead(s)
* Developers
* Agile Champion (optional)
* Business Product Owner (optional)
* DevOps (optional)

## Apps

* Outlook M365 Group Calendar
  * Meeting
  * Date/time
* Slack
  * Reminder with Webex link
* Webex
  * Screen sharing

## Format

| Topic | Duration |  |
| :-------- | :-------- | :-------- |
| Architecture | 5 - 10 min | Highlight of recent architecture policies, decisions, and designs |
| Releases | 5 min | Past & upcoming releases relevant to team |
| Incidents | 10 - 15 min | Review of incidents past 2 weeks relevant to team |
| Root Cause Analysis | late IST, early MST |
| Tooling | 5 - 10 min | 
| Knowledge | 5 min | Reposting of new knowledge |
| User Stories | 10 - 15 min | Open forum and discussion on technical aspects of User Stories |
