Title Blocks

Drawings & mockups with Title Blocks help everyone.

For 200+ years draftsman have put Title Blocks in physical architecture drawings.

 * Why can't digital authors do the same?

Benefits
 * documentation
 * attribution to author
 * drawing ID
 * notes & research
 * links
 * access
 * faster review & feedback
 * on-the-fly alternate proposals
 * collaboration

For more see,
* https://parkdesigns.github.io/diagrams/
