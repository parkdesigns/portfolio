BIMI

Security & Usability in GUI is paramount. BIMI adds these.

BIMI - Brand Indicators for Message Identification (see bimigroup.org)
BIMI is more than just a trademarked logo.

BIMI provides:
* authentication
* anti-phishing
* branding
* confidence
* readability
