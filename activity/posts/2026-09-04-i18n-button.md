i18n - Internationalization

GUI design MUST provide for languages other than English.

In ~2017 one of my first contributions to the Amex Chat UI was to move away from an image-driven button to a CSS-driven button.

CSS-driven design == Lot's of benefits

However, with regards to i18n I could never convince business to use any other word than "Chat" for the Chat button.
* Chinese - `聊天`
* English - `Chat`
* Japanese - `チャット`
* Korean - `채팅`
* Portugese - `Bate-papo`
* Swedish - `Chatta`

I tried especially hard to get "チャット" for Japanese users.

Though most Japanese users type via keyboard using Romanji, ie "c h a t t o", and probably think "Chat" I still feel the english word by itself breaks from the entire UI of Japanese (Hiragana, Katakana, and Kanji).

What do you think?
